Why consultants lose mandates before the brief goes out

Most consultants post when a project ends and the pipeline needs attention. The habit is understandable. It also means your name is missing during the weeks when a buyer forms a shortlist.

The 6sense 2025 B2B Buyer Experience Report drew on nearly 4,000 buyer responses and found that buyers choose from one of the four vendors on their Day One shortlist, nearly every time. If you are not one of the four, the brief is written for someone else.

Consistency is the hard part when you are either billing your time or selling it. A consultant with a full diary has plenty of ideas. The diary leaves no room to write them down.

A fixed call every two weeks takes the writing out of the process, so visibility no longer waits for a quiet week.

Why LinkedIn works differently when you work for yourself

An employee borrows credibility from the firm behind them. As an independent, the credibility is yours alone, so generic content does little for your commercial position.

What works is specific: an observation from a recent engagement, a finding that contradicts the usual advice, a way of framing a problem that makes a prospective client feel understood before you have spoken. That material comes out of conversation, so the service starts with a call.

Your perspective on the work is the one thing a competitor cannot copy.

Common questions about LinkedIn personal branding for management consultants

Does LinkedIn generate work for independent consultants?

Yes, mainly through referrals and shortlists. Regular posts keep you visible to your existing network, which reactivates quiet relationships, and they give new contacts evidence of your thinking when a referral sends them to your profile. Buyers form their shortlist before they brief anyone, so being known beforehand matters.

How long does LinkedIn personal branding take to work for a consultant?

Early signals usually appear within 8 to 10 weeks: more relevant profile views, connection requests from people you would want to work with, and the occasional direct message. Consistent commercial impact typically builds over 6 to 12 months.

I am not a natural writer. Does that matter?

No. The Content Call is built for people who have plenty to say and no wish to face a blank page. You talk, we write, and nothing is published until you have approved it. Your voice, your thinking, your name on it.

How often should a consultant post on LinkedIn?

Two to three times a week suits most consultants. That cadence keeps you in front of your network without becoming a second job, and one 45-minute call every two weeks produces 10 to 13 pieces, which is enough to sustain it.

What should a consultant post on LinkedIn?

Specific material performs best. Observations from recent engagements, anonymised, counterintuitive findings from your field, frameworks that solve problems clients commonly face, and honest commentary on sector trends from someone who has seen inside the organisations involved.
