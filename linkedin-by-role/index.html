LinkedIn content agency and personal branding, by role

The visibility challenge for an independent consultant is not the same as it is for a SaaS founder or a private equity partner. Here is how Blueberry Media approaches each one.

Blueberry Media works with eight B2B roles, each facing a different LinkedIn visibility problem. Consultants and professional services leaders need reputation that reaches beyond referrals. Founders and PE partners need pre-contact credibility with buyers and investors. Sales leaders and agency owners need a voice that opens doors their teams cannot. Every role runs on the same underlying service: a bi-weekly recorded conversation turned into content, tailored to the specific credibility gap each audience faces.

Every role on this list shares the same core problem: the people most likely to hire you are forming opinions before you know they exist. What differs is the specific way that plays out, and what good LinkedIn content looks like as a result.

Common questions

How is LinkedIn strategy different across roles?

The mechanics are similar, but the audience and proof points differ. A consultant needs to demonstrate independent judgement to win mandates. A SaaS founder needs to build credibility with both enterprise buyers and investors at once. A fractional executive needs to signal availability without looking underemployed. The content strategy is built around the specific credibility gap each role faces.

I don't see my exact role listed. Does this still apply to me?

Yes. The eight roles here cover the most common patterns we see, but the underlying service, a bi-weekly recorded conversation turned into LinkedIn content, works for any B2B founder, executive, or leader who wants visibility without writing it themselves. Book a call and we will talk through your specific situation.

How do I know which role page is most relevant to me?

Pick the one closest to how buyers or investors currently evaluate you. Many clients sit across two categories, for example a fractional CFO who is also effectively a consultant. Either page reflects the same underlying service, so start with whichever framing feels closest and we will refine it together.
