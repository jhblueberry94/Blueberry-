LinkedIn personal branding, by role and sector

Buyers, boards and investors each check different things before they get in touch. Here is how Blueberry Media approaches each role and sector.

LinkedIn personal branding works differently for each role and sector, because each audience checks different things before they contact you. Blueberry Media runs the same service for all of them: a 45-minute recorded call every two weeks, turned into LinkedIn content in your voice and shaped around the credibility gap your buyers see.

Which page fits you?

Start with the question that matches how people decide whether to work with you:

  • People hire you personally, for a mandate, an advisory role or a seat at a deal: start with consultants, fractional executives, professional services leaders, private equity partners or agency owners.
  • Buyers, boards or investors judge a company through you: start with B2B founders, SaaS founders, sales leaders, CMOs, Heads of Growth or CROs.
  • You sell into a regulated or technical sector: start with fintech or cybersecurity.

Common questions

Last updated 19 October 2026

How is LinkedIn strategy different across roles?

The mechanics are similar, but the audience and proof points differ. A consultant needs to demonstrate independent judgement to win mandates. A SaaS founder needs to build credibility with both enterprise buyers and investors at once. A fractional executive needs to signal availability without looking underemployed. The content strategy is built around the specific credibility gap each role faces.

I don't see my exact role listed. Does this still apply to me?

Yes. The eleven roles here cover the most common patterns we see, but the underlying service, a bi-weekly recorded conversation turned into LinkedIn content, works for any B2B founder, executive, or leader who wants visibility without writing it themselves. Book a call and we will talk through your specific situation.

How do I know which role page is most relevant to me?

Pick the one closest to how buyers or investors currently evaluate you. Many clients sit across two categories, for example a fractional CFO who is also effectively a consultant. Either page reflects the same underlying service, so start with whichever framing feels closest and we will refine it together.

What is the difference between a role page and a sector page?

A role page covers the credibility gap a particular job faces, such as a CRO or a fractional executive. A sector page covers what buyers in a particular industry check before they trust a provider, such as risk and regulation in fintech or technical depth in cybersecurity. The service underneath is the same.

LinkedIn personal branding packages start from £1,000 per month. Every engagement starts with an initial 90-day sprint, then moves to monthly rolling with 30 days notice. There are no setup fees and no exit penalties.
