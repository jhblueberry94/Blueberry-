Winnersh, Thames Valley

A life sciences and technology cluster at Winnersh Triangle, and the LinkedIn gap

Winnersh is best known in B2B circles as the location of Winnersh Triangle, a major business park that has developed into a genuine science and technology hub. Employers based there include Jacobs, Hewlett Packard, and Hollister, alongside A. Menarini Diagnostics, which has its headquarters on the park, and BD, the global medical technology company. Around half of the buildings on the park combine office space with laboratories, data centres, and manufacturing units rather than standard office floors alone.

That mix of life sciences, medical technology, and engineering-led businesses means Winnersh-based B2B founders are often selling into a technically sophisticated buyer base who expect genuine domain expertise rather than generic marketing messaging. LinkedIn is where those buyers research providers before making contact, and a credible, consistent presence is what makes an independent founder or specialist consultancy visible during that phase.

Blueberry Media is based around 24 miles away in Hungerford, Berkshire, within the same Thames Valley business community. We understand the technical, science-led character of Winnersh's economy and what buyers in that world are actually looking for when they research suppliers on LinkedIn.

Who we work with in Winnersh

Our Winnersh-area clients tend to be founders, consultants, and senior specialists in life sciences, medical technology, diagnostics, engineering, and data or AI-driven business services, several of them operating from or alongside the Winnersh Triangle science and technology cluster.

Many have built genuine technical authority in specialist, often regulated fields, but have not found a way to reflect that expertise anywhere buyers can find it outside their existing network. LinkedIn, built from real expertise and delivered consistently, is the most direct way to put that authority in front of new buyers in life sciences and technology-adjacent markets.

How the service works

Every engagement starts with a 45-minute recorded conversation every two weeks. We send you a set of questions in advance. You talk naturally about your market, your experience, and your current thinking. From that conversation, we build three to four LinkedIn posts per week that reflect your voice accurately, because the material comes directly from you.

Every package includes profile optimisation, a content strategy built around your commercial goals, and a monthly performance report covering impressions, profile views, connection growth, and inbound signals. Higher-tier packages add newsletter writing, outreach management, and short-form video production from your recorded conversation.

The service is delivered entirely remotely. You do not need to travel to a meeting or adjust your schedule beyond the fortnightly recording call. Everything else is handled.
