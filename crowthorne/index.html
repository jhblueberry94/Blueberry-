Crowthorne, Thames Valley

A transport engineering research base, and the LinkedIn gap

Crowthorne's economy is shaped by three long-standing institutions: the Transport Research Laboratory, which has been based in the town since the 1960s and is now its main non-commuting employer, Wellington College, the large independent boarding and day school founded in 1859, and Broadmoor Hospital, one of England's three maximum-security psychiatric hospitals. Together they give a small, largely residential town a genuinely technical and institutional character alongside its commuter-village feel.

That combination means Crowthorne-based B2B founders, especially those in engineering, transport technology, and specialist technical consultancy, are often selling into a buyer base that expects real domain expertise. LinkedIn is where those buyers do their research before making contact, and a credible, consistent presence is what makes an independent specialist visible during that phase, rather than relying solely on local reputation in a small town.

Blueberry Media is based around 26 miles away in Hungerford, Berkshire, within the same Thames Valley and Bracknell Forest business community. We understand the technical, research-led character of Crowthorne's economy and what buyers in that world are looking for on LinkedIn.

Who we work with in Crowthorne

Our Crowthorne-area clients tend to be founders, consultants, and senior specialists in engineering, transport and infrastructure research, education-adjacent services, and specialist technical or scientific consultancy, several with experience connected to the Transport Research Laboratory's work.

Many have built genuine technical authority through years of specialist work but have no consistent way of putting that expertise in front of buyers beyond their existing network. LinkedIn, built from real expertise and delivered consistently, is the most direct way to change that.

How the service works

Every engagement starts with a 45-minute recorded conversation every two weeks. We send you a set of questions in advance. You talk naturally about your market, your experience, and your current thinking. From that conversation, we build three to four LinkedIn posts per week that reflect your voice accurately, because the material comes directly from you.

Every package includes profile optimisation, a content strategy built around your commercial goals, and a monthly performance report covering impressions, profile views, connection growth, and inbound signals. Higher-tier packages add newsletter writing, outreach management, and short-form video production from your recorded conversation.

The service is delivered entirely remotely. You do not need to travel to a meeting or adjust your schedule beyond the fortnightly recording call. Everything else is handled.
