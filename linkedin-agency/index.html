How the service works

The process starts with a 45-minute recorded conversation every two weeks. We send you questions in advance. You talk naturally about your market, your experience, and your thinking. From that conversation, we build three to four LinkedIn posts per week that sound exactly like you, because they come directly from you.

Every package includes profile optimisation, a content strategy built around your commercial goals, and a monthly performance report covering impressions, profile views, connection growth, and any inbound signals. Higher-tier packages add newsletter writing, outreach management, and video clip production. See the full breakdown on our services page.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Last updated 11 September 2026

Is Blueberry Media a national agency or a local one? +

Both, honestly. We're based in Hungerford, Berkshire, and started by building a strong local reputation there. The same remote-first model that works for Berkshire clients works for founders and directors anywhere in the UK.

Do you only work with clients in the cities listed on this page? +

No. The cities listed are where we have the deepest local context and the most client experience so far, not a restriction. We work with founders and directors across the UK.

What is a LinkedIn personal branding agency, and how is it different from a marketing agency? +

A LinkedIn personal branding agency builds a founder or leader's own voice and presence on LinkedIn, rather than a company brand or paid campaign. We are not a general digital marketing agency; we do one thing, done-for-you LinkedIn personal branding, built from a recorded conversation.

How much does a LinkedIn personal branding agency cost? +

Cost depends on scope, from ongoing content retainers to one-off strategy work, and doesn't change based on where in the UK you're based.

What is the contract commitment? +

We start with an initial 90-day sprint, then move to monthly rolling with 30 days notice. No long-term lock-ins, no setup fees, no exit penalties.
