Lever Brands alternatives: how to choose a UK LinkedIn personal branding agency

Lever Brands is a London personal branding agency for founders and CEOs, with services that span LinkedIn, video, newsletters and offers. Alternatives suit different needs, so compare on scope, published pricing, contract terms and sector fit. People searching for Lever Brands alternatives are usually at the shortlist stage. They have come across one agency, perhaps read its guides, and want to know who else does the same work and how to tell the options apart. This guide sets out what Lever Brands says it offers, the criteria worth comparing any agency on, and where Blueberry Media fits.

We are an agency in this market, so this is not a neutral review. Every statement about Lever Brands comes from its own website, which we read in October 2026, and is worded that way. Where we could not find something, such as pricing, we say so. Our wider comparison of nine UK agencies is in LinkedIn personal branding agency UK: 9 agencies compared.

What Lever Brands says it offers

According to its services page, Lever Brands offers done-for-you personal branding for founders and CEOs, grouped into three areas. Attention covers personal brand strategy, LinkedIn management, short-form video, content distribution and LinkedIn outreach. Nurture covers newsletters, lead magnets, funnels and YouTube management. Monetisation covers offer development, which its FAQ says can include digital products and group programmes.

Its About page describes a founder-led agency in London Bridge, with a team of brand managers, community managers and designers working on client accounts. The site does not state pricing or contract length, and it asks visitors to share their goals before it recommends services. Its homepage FAQ says most clients spend around an hour a week.

When Lever Brands may suit you better

Lever Brands may be the better fit if you want one agency to run several channels at once, such as LinkedIn, short-form video, YouTube and newsletters, or if you want help turning an audience into products and programmes. It is also a London team, which can matter if you prefer to work face to face. Its stated focus is founders and CEOs across industries.

Questions to ask before you sign with any agency

  1. Can I see the full price and the minimum term in writing?
  2. Who will interview me, and who will write and publish?
  3. How much of my time do you need each month?
  4. Do I approve every post before it goes live?
  5. What does a typical month of output look like?
  6. How do you count results, and can I speak to a current client?

A good agency will answer all six without hesitation. We will, and you can ask us on a free call. Treat headline numbers, such as total impressions or revenue attributed to content, as claims to check: ask what is counted, over what period and for which clients.
