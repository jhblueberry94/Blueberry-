How it works

Four stages, from first call to a compounding engine.

01Weeks 1 to 2

Strategy & ICP alignment

The foundation everything else is built on. We define exactly who you're talking to, what you stand for, and how your content should feel, before a single post goes live.

  • ICP mapping
  • Messaging framework
  • Cadence planning
Live
02Every 2 weeks, 45 minutes

The Content Interview

A structured 45-minute recorded conversation every two weeks. We send questions in advance, you talk naturally, and we record, transcribe and extract everything worth publishing.

  • No scripting needed
  • Full transcript
  • Content extraction
03Within 14 days of first call

Create & Refine

We edit, write and polish everything in your voice, not ours. Posts, video clips and newsletter or article content, all produced from a single conversation. You review and approve before anything goes live.

  • 3 to 4 posts/week
  • Unlimited refinements
  • Your approval
04Ongoing, every month

Distribute & build visibility

We publish at optimal times, manage connection outreach to your ICP, track performance, and send a monthly report. You stay visible without lifting a finger.

  • ICP outreach
  • Monthly report
  • Data-led refinement

Is this right for you

Built for leaders with limited time.

This works well for a specific kind of client. Here's an honest read before you book a call.

This is for you if
  • You're a founder, exec or consultant running a team of 6–20 people, or working independently, and buyers already research you before they call
  • You have genuine expertise and opinions, but no time or inclination to write posts yourself
  • You want visibility that compounds over months, not a one-off campaign
  • You'd rather talk for 45 minutes every two weeks than sit down to write
Probably not yet if
  • You want a single viral post, not an ongoing presence
  • You're not willing to review and approve content before it goes live
  • Your buyers genuinely aren't active on LinkedIn
  • You're looking for the cheapest option in the market rather than the right fit

FAQ

Questions people ask before choosing an agency.

Last updated 30 September 2026

A LinkedIn personal branding agency builds and manages your LinkedIn presence for you: strategy, content, posting and reporting, so you show up consistently without writing anything yourself. At Blueberry, that means a bi-weekly recorded conversation turned into 10 or more posts, clips and articles, published in your voice.

A freelancer or AI tool works from a brief or a prompt, which is why the output often reads as generic. We work from a recorded conversation with you, so the content comes from your own words and thinking rather than someone else's interpretation of a brief.
