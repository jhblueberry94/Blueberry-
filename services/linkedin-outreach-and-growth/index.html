FAQ

Questions people ask before starting outreach.

Last updated 30 September 2026

A LinkedIn outreach and growth service builds a targeted prospect list and runs personalised connection campaigns on your behalf, so pipeline conversations start without you spending hours prospecting. At Blueberry, that means an ICP onboarding call, a sourced and qualified lead list, and ongoing connection campaigns run in your voice.

Automation tools send templated connection requests and follow-ups at scale, which is exactly the pattern LinkedIn's own systems are built to detect and restrict. We run every campaign manually, with a real person writing a personalised note that references something true about each prospect, so messages stay within LinkedIn's limits and actually get replies.

Pricing depends on list volume and campaign complexity, so we quote it properly on a call rather than guessing at a number here. Pricing in this category varies widely, so it's worth asking any provider for their real monthly volume and minimum contract length before comparing on price alone.

Yes. Every campaign is run manually within LinkedIn's connection and messaging limits, not through automation software that risks your account. Messages are written from your ICP onboarding call, in your voice and tone, and you review the messaging framework before anything goes out.

Most clients spend under two hours a month: the initial ICP onboarding call, then a brief monthly review of the lead list and campaign performance. We handle the connection requests, follow-ups and reporting.

A 90-day initial sprint, then rolling 30-day terms with 30 days notice, the same model used across Blueberry's services. No 6 or 12-month lock-in, no setup fees, no exit penalties.

Connection acceptances build from the first campaign, typically within the first couple of weeks. Replies and warmer conversations follow as the list works through, with meaningful pipeline signals typically visible at 60 to 90 days.
