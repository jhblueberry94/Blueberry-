London, National Hub

The UK's most competitive market, and the LinkedIn gap inside it

London's financial district is going through its most visible expansion in years. JPMorgan Chase announced plans in November 2025 for a new Canary Wharf headquarters tower, three million square feet and 12,000 employees, one of the largest single office commitments in the City's history. Barclays' global headquarters sits at One Churchill Place in Canary Wharf, and HSBC continues to hold a major presence there even as its own headquarters shifts to the City of London.

London has the highest business density of any UK region, with 1,436 businesses per 10,000 adults and roughly 1.04 million registered businesses in total (Gov.uk, Business Population Estimates for the UK and Regions, October 2025). 9.4% of City of London businesses were classified as high growth in 2023, against 4.9% nationally (ONS, High Growth Businesses, 2024). This is the same buyer research behaviour covered in our guide to B2B buying behaviour on LinkedIn — buyers are researching before they ever make contact, and London's density just raises the stakes on being visible during that phase.

Blueberry Media is based around 65 miles away in Hungerford, Berkshire. We work with London-based founders and directors entirely remotely, the same way we work with clients across the UK.

Who we work with in London

Our London clients tend to sit in professional and financial services, consultancy, and B2B SaaS, reflecting the sectors concentrated around Canary Wharf and the City. Many have built real credibility through their work but have no consistent way of putting that expertise in front of buyers who are already researching on LinkedIn before they ever make contact.

How the service works

Every engagement starts with a 45-minute recorded conversation every two weeks. We send you a set of questions in advance. You talk naturally about your market, your experience, and your current thinking. From that conversation, we build three to four LinkedIn posts per week that reflect your voice accurately, because the material comes directly from you.

Every package includes profile optimisation, a content strategy built around your commercial goals, and a monthly performance report covering impressions, profile views, connection growth, and inbound signals. Higher-tier packages add newsletter writing, outreach management, and short-form video production from your recorded conversation.

The service is delivered entirely remotely. You do not need to travel to a meeting or adjust your schedule beyond the fortnightly recording call. See our LinkedIn personal branding service for the full breakdown.

Does Blueberry Media have an office in London? +

No. Blueberry Media is based in Hungerford, Berkshire, around 65 miles from London, and works with London-based founders and directors entirely remotely. There is no local office and no local team, just the same direct working relationship you'd get either way.

Why would a London-based founder use a remote LinkedIn agency? +

LinkedIn personal branding doesn't depend on geography. It depends on understanding your voice, your sector and your buyers well enough to write like you, not like a template. That work happens over a recorded conversation and written briefs, not in person.

What sectors in London does Blueberry Media typically work with? +

Most London clients sit in professional and financial services, consultancy, and B2B SaaS, reflecting the sectors concentrated around Canary Wharf and the City.

How much does LinkedIn personal branding support cost for a London-based founder? +

Pricing depends on scope, from ongoing content retainers to one-off strategy work, and doesn't change based on being in London specifically.

No. We start with an initial 90-day sprint, then move to monthly rolling with 30 days notice. No setup fees, no exit penalties, no minimum term.
