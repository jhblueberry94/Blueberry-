The Northern Square Mile, and the LinkedIn gap inside it

Leeds' financial district had a busy September. On 21 September 2026 the Bank of England announced a new office at Capitol House in Bond Court, which it plans to move into in late 2028, adding to the just under 300 colleagues already based in the city. A week earlier, LHV Bank moved into larger premises at Wellington Place, which is also home to Lloyds Banking Group, Rathbones and Equifax, and said it chose the space for its proximity to technical talent. The National Wealth Fund, the government-backed investor that grew out of the UK Infrastructure Bank, is headquartered in Leeds, and in July 2025 the Chancellor unveiled the Leeds Reforms to UK financial services in the city.

The Mayor of West Yorkshire describes the region's financial services sector as the largest in England outside London, with around 30,000 firms employing almost 300,000 people (West Yorkshire Combined Authority, July 2025). Those figures cover the whole of West Yorkshire. Tech is growing alongside finance: The Data City counts 853 tech companies based in Leeds, more than Manchester, Edinburgh or Birmingham, with 13% growing by more than 20% a year and a combined £1.67bn in gross value added (reported by The Yorkshire Press, September 2026).

A buyer choosing between a Leeds adviser and a City firm often looks at the people as well as the firm, and a partner's or director's LinkedIn profile is a common place for that comparison to start. Our guide to personal branding for professional services leaders looks at how that first impression forms.

Blueberry Media is based in Hungerford, Berkshire, around 203 miles from Leeds by road. We work with Leeds-based founders and directors entirely remotely, the same way we work with clients across the UK.

Leeds by district: where the buyers actually are

Leeds city centre is compact, but its business districts each have their own character. The address on a founder's email signature often hints at the clients they're going after.

District Dominant sector Why it matters for LinkedIn
Park Row, Bond Court and the central business district Financial and professional services The Bank of England's new office at Capitol House, due in late 2028, sits in this core. Partners and directors here often sell on reputation, and a considered LinkedIn presence carries that reputation beyond the people who already know them.
Wellington Place Financial services, fintech and public sector LHV Bank, Lloyds Banking Group, Rathbones and Equifax are based here, alongside the government hub at 7 and 8 Wellington Place, built for HMRC and NHS Digital. In organisations of that size, several people may look up a supplier's founder before anyone agrees to a call.
Leeds Dock Digital, media and creative Leeds Dock is a hub for digital, media and creative businesses, and Sky has digital and technology teams here. Founder-led agencies often win work on the founder's name, so the founder's own posts can carry much of the business development.

Who we work with in Leeds

This service suits partners in law and accountancy firms, leaders of wealth, advisory and fintech businesses, and founders of digital and creative agencies in Leeds. Firms that have grown on referrals often want to be found by buyers who haven't met them yet, in London and across the North as well as in Yorkshire. Each fortnight we talk through recent deals, cases and client questions, and ghostwrite posts that sound like you in a client meeting: specific and useful to whoever is reading.

How the service works

Every engagement starts with a 45-minute recorded conversation every two weeks. We send you a set of questions in advance. You talk naturally about your market, your experience, and your current thinking. From that conversation, we build three to four LinkedIn posts per week that reflect your voice accurately, because the material comes directly from you.

Every package includes profile optimisation, a content strategy built around your commercial goals, and a monthly performance report covering impressions, profile views, connection growth, and inbound signals. Higher-tier packages add newsletter writing, outreach management, and short-form video production from your recorded conversation.

The service is delivered entirely remotely. You do not need to travel to a meeting or adjust your schedule beyond the fortnightly recording call. See our LinkedIn personal branding service for the full breakdown.

Does Blueberry Media have an office in Leeds? +

No. Blueberry Media is based in Hungerford, Berkshire, around 203 miles from Leeds by road, and works with Leeds-based founders and directors entirely remotely, as we do with clients across the UK. The fortnightly conversation is a recorded call, so there is nothing to attend in person.

Why would a Leeds-based founder use a remote LinkedIn agency? +

The work behind LinkedIn personal branding is understanding your voice, your sector and your buyers well enough to write as you would. That happens over a recorded conversation and written briefs, so it works the same from Leeds as from anywhere else.

Which sectors in Leeds does Blueberry Media work with? +

We focus on partners and directors in professional and financial services, around the central business district and Wellington Place, and on founders of digital and creative agencies near Leeds Dock.

How much does LinkedIn personal branding support cost for a Leeds-based founder? +

Pricing depends on scope, from ongoing content retainers to one-off strategy work, and is the same wherever you are based. Get in touch with a sense of what you need and we'll give you a straight answer.

Is there a long-term contract? +

Not long term. We start with an initial 90-day sprint, then move to monthly rolling with 30 days notice, with no setup fees and no exit penalties. See how the process works.
