Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 July 2026

Blueberry Media Digital Ltd ("Blueberry Media", "we", "us") provides LinkedIn content and thought leadership services for B2B founders and executives. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, and the choices and rights you have.

Who we are

Blueberry Media is based in Hungerford, Berkshire, United Kingdom. We are the data controller for the personal information described in this policy. If you have a question about how we handle your data, you can reach us using the details at the bottom of this page.

Information we collect

We collect information directly from you, and some information automatically when you visit our website.

  • Contact form: your name, email address, company name and the message you send us.
  • Newsletter sign-up: your email address, so we can send you content updates. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • Call bookings: your name, email and any details you provide when scheduling a call with us.
  • Client content calls: if you become a client, we record and transcribe our content calls in order to produce your LinkedIn content. These recordings are used solely for content production and internal reference.
  • Website usage: we use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our site, including pages viewed, time on site and general location. This data is aggregated and does not directly identify you.

How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Respond to enquiries and questions you send us
  • Deliver the services you have engaged us for
  • Send you content, updates or marketing communications you have opted into
  • Understand and improve how our website performs
  • Meet our legal and accounting obligations

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Cookies and analytics

Our website uses Google Analytics 4 to measure site traffic and usage patterns. This involves cookies that collect standard log information and visitor behaviour in an anonymised or pseudonymised form. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking using your browser settings or a browser extension such as Google's Analytics opt-out add-on.

Sharing your information

We share personal information with a small number of trusted service providers who help us run our business, including our email delivery provider, customer relationship management software, meeting and content production tools, and website hosting provider. These providers only process your data on our instructions and are required to keep it secure. We do not share your information with anyone else unless required by law.

Where your data is stored

Some of the service providers we use may store or process data outside the United Kingdom, including in the United States. Where this happens, we rely on providers that maintain appropriate safeguards for international data transfers, such as the UK's International Data Transfer Addendum or equivalent standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep your data

We keep enquiry and marketing data for as long as you remain subscribed or until you ask us to delete it. Client data, including call recordings and content, is kept for the duration of our working relationship and for a reasonable period afterwards to meet contractual and legal obligations, after which it is securely deleted.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate information
  • Ask us to delete your information
  • Object to or restrict how we use your information
  • Ask for your information in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent to marketing at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below. If you are not satisfied with how we have handled your information, you have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

Children's privacy

Our services are directed at businesses and professionals. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised.

Contact us

If you have any questions about this policy or how we handle your information, please get in touch via our contact page.
