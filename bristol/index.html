Bristol, National Hub

The South West's largest economy, and the LinkedIn gap inside it

Bristol's professional services sector is going through a visible wave of relocation. Hargreaves Lansdown is moving from its Anchor Road headquarters into 90,000 sq ft across three floors of Temple Quay's Welcome Building, alongside DAC Beachcroft and Unite. Lloyds Banking Group is consolidating staff into a refurbished Bristol office as it closes its former Gloucester site, and legal and architecture firms Shakespeare Martineau and Marrons have both moved their Bristol operations to One Temple Quay in the past two years.

Bristol's business base expanded by 11% between 2015 and 2022, and micro-enterprises made up 87% of that base by 2022 (Bristol City Council, Economic Strategy 2025–2035, sourced to ONS/IDBR). The city's most consistent start-up sectors are real estate and professional services, wholesale and retail, and transport and communications. That growth mirrors the buyer research behaviour covered in our guide to B2B buying behaviour on LinkedIn. A denser founder-led business base means more buyers forming an opinion of a supplier before any direct contact happens.

Blueberry Media is based around 55 miles away in Hungerford, Berkshire. We work with Bristol-based founders and directors entirely remotely, the same way we work with clients across the UK.

Who we work with in Bristol

Our Bristol clients tend to sit in professional and financial services around Temple Quarter, creative and tech businesses on the Harbourside, and boutique consultancies based in Clifton. A lot of that credibility currently lives in conversations, pitches and closed deals rather than somewhere a prospective client can actually find it. We give it a public home on LinkedIn, written in your own words, so the research a Temple Quarter buyer or a Harbourside client does before ever getting in touch reflects how good you actually are.

How the service works

Every engagement starts with a 45-minute recorded conversation every two weeks. We send you a set of questions in advance. You talk naturally about your market, your experience, and your current thinking. From that conversation, we build three to four LinkedIn posts per week that reflect your voice accurately, because the material comes directly from you.

Every package includes profile optimisation, a content strategy built around your commercial goals, and a monthly performance report covering impressions, profile views, connection growth, and inbound signals. Higher-tier packages add newsletter writing, outreach management, and short-form video production from your recorded conversation.

The service is delivered entirely remotely. You do not need to travel to a meeting or adjust your schedule beyond the fortnightly recording call. See our LinkedIn personal branding service for the full breakdown.

Does Blueberry Media have an office in Bristol? +

No. Blueberry Media is based in Hungerford, Berkshire, around 55 miles from Bristol, and works with Bristol-based founders and directors entirely remotely. There is no local office and no local team, just the same direct working relationship you'd get either way.

Why would a Bristol-based founder use a remote LinkedIn agency? +

LinkedIn personal branding doesn't depend on geography. It depends on understanding your voice, your sector and your buyers well enough to write like you, not like a template. That work happens over a recorded conversation and written briefs, not in person.

What sectors in Bristol does Blueberry Media typically work with? +

Most Bristol clients sit in professional and financial services, creative and tech, and boutique consultancy, reflecting the mix of businesses across Temple Quarter, the Harbourside and Clifton.

How much does LinkedIn personal branding support cost for a Bristol-based founder? +

Pricing depends on scope, from ongoing content retainers to one-off strategy work, and doesn't change based on being in Bristol specifically. Get in touch with a sense of what you need and we'll give you a straight answer.

Is there a long-term contract? +

No. We start with an initial 90-day sprint, then move to monthly rolling with 30 days notice. No setup fees, no exit penalties, no minimum term.
