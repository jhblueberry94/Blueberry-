Sandhurst, Thames Valley

The Royal Military Academy, a major retail development, and the LinkedIn gap

Sandhurst is known worldwide as the home of the Royal Military Academy Sandhurst, the British Army's officer training centre, which sits within the town even though its ceremonial entrance faces neighbouring Camberley in Surrey. Alongside the Academy, Sandhurst is home to The Meadows, a large out-of-town retail and commercial development that anchors much of the town's day-to-day commercial activity.

That combination of a globally recognised institution and a genuinely local, largely residential business base means Sandhurst-based B2B founders and consultants often serve clients well beyond the town itself, competing for attention against much larger, better-known businesses in neighbouring Camberley and Bracknell. A consistent LinkedIn presence is one of the more direct ways an independent founder can build visibility that does not depend on the town's own limited commercial footprint.

Blueberry Media is based around 28 miles away in Hungerford, Berkshire, within the same Thames Valley and Bracknell Forest business community. We understand the mix of institutional and independent business activity that shapes Sandhurst's economy.

Who we work with in Sandhurst

Our Sandhurst-area clients tend to be founders and consultants in professional services, training and education-adjacent businesses, and retail or consumer-facing enterprises connected to The Meadows and the wider Bracknell Forest borough.

Many serve clients across Berkshire, Surrey, and Hampshire rather than relying solely on Sandhurst's own commercial base, which makes a platform like LinkedIn, that is not bound by location, particularly valuable for reaching buyers outside the immediate area.

How the service works

Every engagement starts with a 45-minute recorded conversation every two weeks. We send you a set of questions in advance. You talk naturally about your market, your experience, and your current thinking. From that conversation, we build three to four LinkedIn posts per week that reflect your voice accurately, because the material comes directly from you.

Every package includes profile optimisation, a content strategy built around your commercial goals, and a monthly performance report covering impressions, profile views, connection growth, and inbound signals. Higher-tier packages add newsletter writing, outreach management, and short-form video production from your recorded conversation.

The service is delivered entirely remotely. You do not need to travel to a meeting or adjust your schedule beyond the fortnightly recording call. Everything else is handled.
