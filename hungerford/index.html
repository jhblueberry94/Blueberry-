Hungerford, Thames Valley

Blueberry Media's home town, a nationally known antiques trade, and the LinkedIn opportunity

Hungerford is best known nationally for its antiques trade: the High Street has one of the best-known concentrations of antiques dealers in the country, alongside a base of independent retail, agricultural and rural businesses, and professional services firms. Charnham Business Park sits just off the A4 close to M4 Junction 14, and the Kennet and Avon Canal runs directly through the town centre.

For B2B founders and consultants based in Hungerford, that mix of independent trade and professional services means most of the town's business owners rely heavily on reputation and word of mouth, with very little consistent LinkedIn presence to back it up. That leaves an open field for anyone willing to show up on the platform consistently.

Blueberry Media is not a short distance from Hungerford. We are based here. This is where the business started, and where we understand the local business community first-hand rather than from a neighbouring town.

Who we work with in Hungerford

Our Hungerford-area clients include founders and specialists in professional and financial advisory services, independent retail and trade businesses, agricultural and rural enterprises, and consultants who serve clients well beyond the town itself from a Hungerford base.

Many have built strong local reputations over years of work but have never translated that credibility into a LinkedIn presence that reaches buyers outside their existing network. Given LinkedIn's reach beyond any single town, that is often the fastest way for a Hungerford-based founder to build a client base across the wider Thames Valley and the UK.

How the service works

Every engagement starts with a 45-minute recorded conversation every two weeks. We send you a set of questions in advance. You talk naturally about your market, your experience, and your current thinking. From that conversation, we build three to four LinkedIn posts per week that reflect your voice accurately, because the material comes directly from you.

Every package includes profile optimisation, a content strategy built around your commercial goals, and a monthly performance report covering impressions, profile views, connection growth, and inbound signals. Higher-tier packages add newsletter writing, outreach management, and short-form video production from your recorded conversation.

The service is delivered entirely remotely. You do not need to travel to a meeting or adjust your schedule beyond the fortnightly recording call. Everything else is handled.
