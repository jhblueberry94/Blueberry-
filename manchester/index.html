Manchester, National Hub

A city in the middle of a relocation wave, and the LinkedIn gap inside it

Manchester city centre is going through one of its most visible waves of relocations in years. The Information Commissioner's Office, the UK's independent data protection regulator, announced in June 2025 that it is moving its national headquarters from Wilmslow, Cheshire, into a 20,000 sq ft space at Bruntwood SciTech's No.3 Circle Square, part of an £87 million, 267,000 sq ft innovation hub on Oxford Road. The move is planned for autumn 2026, ending the regulator's 40-year base in Wilmslow. A few months earlier, in April 2025, BT opened Four New Bailey in Spinningfields, a new multi-million-pound flagship office housing up to 2,000 colleagues, the final step in the group's UK-wide office consolidation programme.

The wider North West region is home to nearly 7 million people and around 230,000 companies, with three-quarters of the UK's Top 100 companies operating somewhere in the region (Department for Business and Trade, business.gov.uk). Greater Manchester's own share of the UK economy has risen to 4.1%, up from 3.6% in 2015, and the city-region has secured £476.7 million in government investment to support further growth (Greater Manchester Combined Authority, January 2026). Manchester's economy is separately forecast to grow at 2.5% a year between 2024 and 2026, the third-fastest rate of any UK city or town, behind only Reading and London (EY ITEM Club, via Greater Manchester Business Board). This is the same buyer research behaviour covered in our guide to B2B buying behaviour on LinkedIn: buyers are researching before they ever make contact, and a city adding this many visible new headquarters just raises the stakes on being visible during that phase.

Blueberry Media is based around 175 miles away in Hungerford, Berkshire. We work with Manchester-based founders and directors entirely remotely, the same way we work with clients across the UK.

How the service works

Every engagement starts with a 45-minute recorded conversation every two weeks. We send you a set of questions in advance. You talk naturally about your market, your experience, and your current thinking. From that conversation, we build three to four LinkedIn posts per week that reflect your voice accurately, because the material comes directly from you.

Every package includes profile optimisation, a content strategy built around your commercial goals, and a monthly performance report covering impressions, profile views, connection growth, and inbound signals. Higher-tier packages add newsletter writing, outreach management, and short-form video production from your recorded conversation.

The service is delivered entirely remotely. You do not need to travel to a meeting or adjust your schedule beyond the fortnightly recording call. See our LinkedIn personal branding service for the full breakdown.

No. Blueberry Media is based in Hungerford, Berkshire, around 175 miles from Manchester, and works with Manchester-based founders and directors entirely remotely. There is no local office and no local team, just the same direct working relationship you'd get either way.

Why would a Manchester-based founder use a remote LinkedIn agency? +

LinkedIn personal branding doesn't depend on geography. It depends on understanding your voice, your sector and your buyers well enough to write like you, not like a template. That work happens over a recorded conversation and written briefs, not in person.

What sectors in Manchester does Blueberry Media typically work with? +

Most Manchester clients sit in professional and financial services, tech and digital, and media and creative, reflecting the sectors concentrated around Spinningfields, the Oxford Road Corridor and MediaCityUK.
